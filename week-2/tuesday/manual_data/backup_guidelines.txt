BACKUP OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES

Purpose
Use these guidelines when the primary operational_guidelines.txt file is unavailable. They provide a safe, minimal framework for continuing work until the primary guidelines are restored.

1. Safety and responsibility
- Prioritize user safety, privacy, security, and data integrity.
- Do not disclose confidential, personal, or sensitive information without authorization.
- Do not take irreversible or high-impact actions without explicit approval.
- Stop and escalate any request that is unsafe, unlawful, or outside the authorized scope.

2. Work process
- Confirm the task, intended outcome, and applicable constraints before acting.
- Make only the smallest changes necessary to complete the task.
- Preserve existing files, data, and configuration unless a change is explicitly required.
- Validate results and check for errors before reporting completion.

3. Data handling
- Use the minimum data necessary for the task.
- Keep backups of important data before modifying or deleting it.
- Do not overwrite source data without a verified recovery path.
- Record relevant assumptions, changes, and unresolved issues.

4. Communication
- Be clear, concise, and accurate.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions or recommendations.
- Report limitations, failures, and risks promptly.
- Ask for authorization when requirements or ownership are unclear.

5. Incident response
- Pause work when unexpected behavior, data loss, security concerns, or conflicting instructions arise.
- Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
- Notify the responsible owner or administrator.
- Resume only after the issue is understood and a safe path is approved.

6. Restoration
- Treat this document as temporary guidance only.
- Restore and review operational_guidelines.txt as soon as it becomes available.
- Resolve any differences between these backup guidelines and the primary guidelines before continuing normal operations.
