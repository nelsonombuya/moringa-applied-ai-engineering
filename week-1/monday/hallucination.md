If an AI says something untrue as if it were a fact, that's called a hallucination.

Imagine a friend who's great at answering questions and telling stories. But when they don't know the answer, instead of saying "I'm not sure," they make up something that sounds believable, with lots of convincing details, without realizing they're wrong. The AI isn't lying, but instead filling in gaps in what it knows with something that sounds right based on patterns it’s learned, even if it’s not true.
